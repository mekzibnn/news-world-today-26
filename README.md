**THE DAY IN FOUR ACTS** — Thursday 26 November 2026 told chronologically:
OVERNIGHT (00–06 UTC) · MORNING (06–12 UTC) · AFTERNOON (12–18 UTC) ·
EVENING (18–24 UTC). 37 stories, 7 regions, every story dated, sourced
and status-tagged. Drawn in code with PIL; no photographs.

  PSG's perfect run · Kyiv hit by ballistic missiles.
- **Morning:** UK-EU bill goes to Parliament · China firmly opposes US
  tariff plan · Kenya court halts US-backed Ebola site · Asia stocks rebound.
- **Afternoon:** Twin storms slam Thanksgiving on both coasts · Yemen
  outlaws Houthis · Australia–Fiji alliance enters into force.
- **Evening:** EU summit ends early with no budget deal · EU leaders back

Plus 24 more stories, a UTC timeline, a 12-item DATE CHECK (what is NOT
26 November), and a HOW SURE legend.
